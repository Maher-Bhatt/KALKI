What it does

Everything you'd ask a good assistant for.

Built for real use on one PC: yours. Each feature says honestly what it did, and what it couldn't.

Talk naturally

A real voice conversation. It notices when you stop, answers out loud, and stops the moment you interrupt. Hindi, English and Hinglish.

Your day, briefed

The first time your PC starts each day, KALKI reads your calendar, reminders, tasks and the weather for where you are.

Music and apps

"Play some music" uses Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music. "Open WhatsApp" opens it, Store apps included.

Tasks, reminders, notes

Repeating reminders with snooze, focus sessions that survive a restart, and searchable notes with tags and Markdown export.

A security check-up

Works with Windows Defender: real status, file checks, and plain reasons when something looks suspicious. Never a made-up malware name.

Remembers you

Facts and preferences you share are kept encrypted on your PC, and used to give answers that fit you.

Voice mode

Like talking to a person.

Open voice mode and just talk. KALKI listens, answers in a natural voice, and keeps the conversation going. Start speaking and it stops to listen.

  • Notices when you've finished, with no button to hold.
  • Understands Hindi, English and Hinglish, with Groq Whisper or free Google speech.
  • Short, spoken answers, with commands like "play some music" that still work.
  • Optional Savage mode: blunt, witty and free to swear. Insults get roasted back in your language.

Privacy and safety

You stay in charge. Here's exactly what happens.

Your chats, notes and memoriesStored on your PC. Memories and keys are encrypted with Windows' own protection (DPAPI).
Your questionsGo to the AI service you choose (Gemini, Groq, OpenAI, Anthropic), or nowhere if you use Ollama offline.
Anything that mattersEmails, deleting things, running commands: KALKI shows exactly what it wants to do and waits for your yes.
Your locationOff until you turn it on. From Windows (GPS or Wi-Fi), never your IP, used only for the weather, and not saved.
Screen timeOff until you turn it on. App names and minutes only, never window titles, with one-click delete.
SecurityKALKI isn't an antivirus. It works with Windows Defender and says plainly when something couldn't be checked.

Questions

Is KALKI free?

Yes. KALKI is free and open source. The AI services have free tiers (Gemini, Groq), or you can run a model offline with Ollama.

Does it need the internet?

Tasks, notes, reminders, focus sessions, music and app control work offline. Answering questions needs an AI service, unless you use Ollama on your PC.

Which languages does it understand?

English, Hindi and Hinglish in both chat and voice. Replies follow the language you use.

Will it do things without asking?

No. Anything consequential, like sending messages, deleting or running commands, waits for your approval, and reading untrusted web content blocks automatic actions for that request.

Does it replace my antivirus?

No. KALKI works with Windows Defender: it shows Defender's real status, checks files with it, and adds a few plain checks of its own.
