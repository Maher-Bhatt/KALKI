## Description (under 10,000 characters)
KALKI is a personal AI assistant for Windows. Talk to it or type, and it helps with your day: answers questions, plans your schedule, sets reminders, plays music, opens apps and keeps an eye on your PC's security. It always asks before doing anything important.

TALK NATURALLY
Say "Hey KALKI" from across the room, or open voice mode and just talk. KALKI notices when you've finished, answers out loud, and stops the moment you interrupt. It understands English, Hindi and Hinglish.

YOUR DAY, BRIEFED
The first time your PC starts each day, KALKI reads you a short briefing: today's calendar, reminders, open tasks and the weather where you are.

GET THINGS DONE
• Reminders that repeat and snooze: "remind me every weekday at 10 to stand up".
• Tasks, and notes you can search, tag and export.
• Focus sessions that keep going even if you restart your PC.
• "Play some music" with Spotify, Apple Music or YouTube Music.
• "Open WhatsApp", "open Spotify": any app on your PC, Microsoft Store apps included.

YOUR CHOICE OF AI
Pick the AI that answers: Google Gemini or Groq (both have free tiers), OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or Ollama to run completely offline. A guided setup shows exactly where to get a key and tests it for you, in about three minutes.

SAFER, WITH WINDOWS DEFENDER
KALKI isn't an antivirus. Its security check-up works with Windows Defender: it shows Defender's real status, checks files with it, explains plainly why something looks suspicious, and can quarantine files safely so they can be restored.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Your chats, notes and memories stay on your PC. Keys and memories are encrypted with Windows' own protection.
• Your questions go only to the AI service you choose, or nowhere if you use Ollama.
• Sending messages, deleting things or running commands always waits for your approval.
• Location and screen time are off until you turn them on.

Optional Savage mode makes KALKI blunt and witty, and it roasts insults back in your language.

KALKI is free and open source (MIT): github.com/Maher-Bhatt/KALKI

## Before you submit
- The demo data in the screenshots (the name Aarav, tasks, calendar, weather) is example content in the real app.
- Partner Center needs a developer account. Check the current registration terms and fees on Microsoft's site.
- For an MSI/EXE listing, Microsoft needs a direct download link to a code-signed installer; unsigned installers may be rejected. The MSIX route can use Store signing instead.
- Check the age rating questionnaire answers: KALKI can produce profanity when Savage mode is on, and connects to the internet.
