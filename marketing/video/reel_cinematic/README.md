# KALKI cinematic Reel

A 21-second, one-take 3D fly-through in the style of a film trailer. KALKI's orb and interface
float in a 3D world; the camera pushes in, orbits, flies *through* the light, glides past the
floating UI, lands on the real app, pulls back and ends on the logo. The score is original and
free to post anywhere.

| File | What it is |
|---|---|
| `kalki_reel_cinematic.mp4` | **Post this one.** 1080×1920, 21 s, with the score. |
| `kalki_reel_cinematic_cover.png` | Cover ("This is KALKI." with the real app). |
| `kalki_reel_cinematic_silent.mp4` | No audio, for your own sound. |
| `kalki_cinematic_score.wav` | The score on its own. |

## The shot
| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 0:00 | Letterboxed darkness, drifting dust, a distant light. "For years, your PC just listened." A low braam |
| 0:02 | "Now, it *answers*." |
| 0:03 | **Ignition:** a second braam, the orb bursts into god rays and a lens flare, the letterbox opens |
| 0:03 to 0:06 | The camera orbits the orb as the conversation appears in 3D, with a rack focus from question to answer |
| 0:06 | **Drop:** taiko drums; the camera flies through the light and past floating cards: "Your day.", "Your music.", "Your PC, protected." |
| 0:10 | It lands on the real app in a Windows 11 window, reflected on a glossy floor: "This is *KALKI*." |
| 0:13 | **Lights out.** Silence. "bro you're kinda useless ngl"… then the roast slams in 💀 |
| 0:15 | The camera pulls back through everything: Free. Open source. Private by design. Built for Windows. |
| 0:17.5 | Everything is pulled into the light; final braam, burst, the logo draws itself through a flare |

**Film look:** depth of field (things off the focus plane blur like a real lens), god rays, anamorphic
lens flares, dust and bokeh, a teal-and-amber grade, vignette, grain, and real motion blur (each
frame blends three moments of the shutter).

**Score:** a low drone and ticking pulse, braams, a string ostinato, taiko drums and big reverb
snares, a riser into each drop, a silence before the roast lands, and a final braam with a chime.
