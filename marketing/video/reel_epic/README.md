## The world
A dawn landscape: layered mountain ranges with haze between them, stars, drifting fog, and a
reflective ground grid running to the horizon. KALKI's orb is the sun.

**Film look:** depth of field, god rays, anamorphic flares, dust and bokeh, a teal-and-amber grade,
vignette, grain, letterboxing and real motion blur (three sub-frames per frame).

**Score:** a dawn drone and ticking pulse, braams, a string ostinato, taiko drums, an airy
half-time "brain" section with chimes, a silence before the roast, a double-time finale and a
final braam with a chime.

## Adding a trending song in Instagram
1. Upload `kalki_reel_epic_sfx.mp4` (or `kalki_reel_epic_silent.mp4` for no effects at all).
2. Tap **Audio**, search the song, and drag its start so **its drop lands at 0:08**, the flash where the
   camera flies through the sun. If the song has a quiet intro, let that play over the sunrise (0:00 to 0:08).
3. Volume: song about 100%, original audio (the effects) about 30 to 40%.
4. Big moments to line up if you trim the song: 0:04 sunrise ignition · 0:08 drop · 0:14.5 the AI ring
   (a calmer part of the song suits it) · 0:18.5 security hit · 0:22 the real app · 0:25.9 silence before the
   roast · 0:31.5 the logo.
