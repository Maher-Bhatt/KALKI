⚙️ UNDER THE HOOD

Desktop Overlay Architecture

Unlike standard web apps or Electron widgets that hog 800 MB of RAM, KALKI's companion pet runs on an ultra-lightweight native wrapper utilizing PyWebView with isolated WebView2 runtime parameters.

It runs as an independent child process (desktop_pet.py) completely decoupled from the main UI window. Even if the main KALKI window is closed, your pet remains on your multi-monitor desktop.
