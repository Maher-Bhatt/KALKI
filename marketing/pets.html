A small friend that shows
what KALKI is doing.

The KALKI pet floats over your other windows. It shows when KALKI is working, when it needs your approval, and when it has finished, so you don't have to keep the app open to know.

Really see-through

No box around it. Clicks on empty space go straight to the window behind.

Approval first

When KALKI needs your OK, the pet shows it before anything else. Click it to review.

Click or drag

Click to ask something or open KALKI. Drag it anywhere on your screen.

Quiet by default

Off until you turn it on, no sound unless you choose it, and still images if your system asks for less motion.

The idea is inspired by Codex pets. The six characters and their art are KALKI's own.
